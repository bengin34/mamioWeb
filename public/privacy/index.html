Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Mamio is designed to keep your baby care data on your device. The app has no user accounts, no login system, and no Mamio server that stores your records. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and in-app purchases are handled by Apple / Google Play with RevenueCat. This policy explains exactly what data those services may receive.

Your baby care data stays on your device

Everything you enter in Mamio is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded to us:

  • Feeding, bottle, pumping, solids, diaper, sleep, and tummy time records, including notes.
  • Growth, health, vaccine, milestone, and doctor records, plus photos and videos you attach.
  • Summaries and statistics generated from those records.
  • App settings, reminders (scheduled as local notifications), and your local Pro status.

If you use the Mamio widget or Apple Watch app, a small summary (for example, the last feed) is shared between the app and the widget/watch on your own devices through Apple's App Groups and WatchConnectivity. It is not sent to us.

Mamio does not collect your name, email address, or account profile, and Mamio itself does not build user profiles or sell data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Pro users do not see ads. Google may collect the following data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement, and reporting:

  • Device and advertising identifiers. On iOS, the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) is only available to Google if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Ad impression and interaction data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped).
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, and device/diagnostic information such as device model, OS version, and app performance data.

Consent (UMP). Where required (for example, in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), Mamio shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice anytime in Mamio under Settings > Privacy & cookie settings.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT). On iOS, Mamio asks for permission through Apple's system prompt with the message "To show relevant ads that help support the free version of Mamio." Permission is optional. If you deny tracking or do not give consent, you can still use Mamio and ads are served as non-personalized ads only. Interstitial and rewarded ads in Mamio are always requested as non-personalized. You can change your ATT choice anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from partner apps (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and manage ad preferences in Google Ad Settings.

Google Firebase

Mamio includes the Google Firebase SDK. Firebase Analytics data collection is turned off in the app: Mamio does not log analytics events, and no analytics data, advertising ID, or vendor ID is sent to Firebase. Mamio does not use Firebase to store or sync your baby care data. Google's privacy information for Firebase is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

Mamio Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore your Pro status. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information (such as product, transaction, and receipt data) together with an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID. RevenueCat does not receive your baby care records, and we do not receive your payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Third-party services at a glance

  • Google AdMob (free version only): ads, ad measurement, consent via UMP. Privacy Policy · Data practices
  • Google Firebase: SDK included, analytics collection disabled. Privacy & security
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation and Pro status. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: payment processing under their own privacy policies.

Exports and backups

If you export a report or a backup file, it is created locally on your device and shared only where you choose (for example, Files, email, or AirDrop). We do not receive a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Your baby care data is kept on your device for as long as you keep it. You can delete individual records in the app, and you can delete all Mamio data by uninstalling the app (or clearing its data on Android). Because we have no server copy, we cannot restore deleted data unless you kept a backup file.

Data collected by Google AdMob and RevenueCat is retained under their own retention policies. Purchase records are also kept by Apple / Google as required for billing. To request deletion of data held by RevenueCat for your anonymous app user ID, or for any privacy question, contact us at the address below and we will help.

Children

Mamio is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children. The data you record is about your baby but is entered by you and remains on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since your baby care data is only on your device, you control it directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
