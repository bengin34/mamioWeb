## Local vs global differences
- Storage times differ clearly (NHS 8 days fridge; DE 4 days; FR 48 h; BR 12 h; TR 4 days). The article lists each and tells readers to follow their own country. Local language follows local guidance (de: Netzwerk GiL, fr: 1000-premiers-jours.fr, pt-br: Ministério da Saúde, tr: ministry hospital pages).
- Session frequency: no national page of DE, UK, BR gives a pumping count. FR and TR numbers are for preterm babies (FR) and supply building (TR) and are labelled as such.

## Open questions for a clinician (if any)
- TR relies on provincial ministry hospital pages (Şiran, İslahiye, Şarkışla, Kocaeli), not a national page. Their numbers (3-hourly; 6 to 12 per day) are not from a national authority; acceptable as listed with attribution.
- Not a clinical question: the Mamio claim in the CTA is deliberately generic because the app facts list has no pumping feature. If a pumping tracker exists in the app, add it to Product facts first.
