## Local vs global differences
- de: screens <3 years "best no screens" (kg-info) stricter than WHO/AAP; stated in text. Hearing screening and late-talker rule (<50 words at 2 y) follow German pages.
- fr: no screens before 3 years (1000 jours, ameli) stricter than WHO/AAP; stated. Hearing: national programme, exams at 4/9/24 months.
- tr: hearing screening 72 h rule follows Sağlık Bakanlığı (Istanbul İSM page).
- pt-br, tr: no national language-milestone or screen-time page could be opened; those sections use CDC/AAP/WHO plus the Caderneta pointer (pt-br).
