## Local vs global differences
- DE: window start of 5th to start of 7th month (not a fixed 6 months); 17-26 weeks. Documented in the article.
- FR: 4 completed months to 6 months (ameli.fr, SPF). Documented.
- BR: about 6 months; SBP acknowledges 4-6 month recommendations. Documented.
- TR: 6 months; 17 / 26 weeks in ASM guide. Documented.
- NHS/CDC/AAP: about 6 months; CDC not before 4 months; AAP weight cue at about 4 months.
