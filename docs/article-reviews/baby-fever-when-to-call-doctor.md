## Local vs global differences
- DE: fever 38.5C (38C first 3 months), doctor if >1 day up to ~2y; medicine usually only >39C rectal. Stricter than NHS for older babies. Stated in de body.
- FR: thresholds >38 rectal, >37.5 axillary; same-day list at >2 days under 2y. Stated in fr body.
- BR: no Brazilian family-facing page opened (ministry pages blocked/404); definition and comfort care from SBP; age thresholds from NHS/AAP. Stated in pt-br body.
- TR: ministry hospital page says any fever under 2y -> doctor without delay; stricter than NHS. Stated in tr body.
- Drug age limits: NHS (paracetamol not <2mo) vs SBP table (paracetamol from neonates >3kg). Pt-br body states both, no doses.
