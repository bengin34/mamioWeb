## Local vs global differences
- Start age: NHS "around 6 months"; AAP 6 months (some ready at 4); kindergesundheit-info.de earliest after the 4th completed month, latest start of the 7th; Santé publique France 4-6 months for diversification (6 months for DME); SBP 6 months; Ministry TR 6th month. Stated in each language.
- Lumps: NHS 6-7 months; SpF 6/8, 8, 10 months; SBP 9-11 months transition and pieces by 10 months; TR Behçet Uz 9-11 months finger foods; German pages give no month for lumps. Stated in de, fr, pt-br, tr.
- Method: German bodies and SBP hold the classic spoon plan as the base and treat BLW/finger food as a complement (SBP: not as the only method); NHS and AAP are more open. Stated.
- Gagging: not covered on any de, fr, pt-br or tr page opened; article says so and uses NHS.

## Open questions for a clinician
- Local emergency numbers (de 112, fr 15 or 112, pt-br SAMU 192, tr 112) come from the skill rule, not from the opened pages.
- Ministry guide (TR) lists raw carrot as a hand-held food while also warning against uncooked carrot; article only cites banana as the example.
- pt-br has no gov.br Ministério da Saúde page opened (SBP is the local official body; MS guidance is only quoted via SBP). Consider adding the Guia Alimentar para crianças menores de 2 anos.
