## Local vs global differences
- Newborn range differs by source: 12-18 h (TR ministry), 14-17 h (CDC), 16-18 h (DE), 16-20 h (SBP), up to 20 h (ameli.fr), ~18 h (NHS). Documented; ranges overlap.
- 1-2 y: CDC 11-14 h vs NHS 12-15 h; article uses the 12-14 h overlap as a rough guide and says so.
- The CDC and SBP tables are adapted from the AASM consensus; the article cites the CDC and SBP pages themselves, not the AASM.
- DE, TR: no age-band hours beyond newborn in the opened official pages, so CDC/NHS are cited for 4-12 months and 1-2 years.
