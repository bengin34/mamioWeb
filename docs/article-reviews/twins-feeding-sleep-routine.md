## Local vs global differences
- Sleep: no German, French, Brazilian or Turkish twin-specific guidance found. HAS (fr) and BIÖG/DGKJ (de) give single-baby rules (own bed, parents' room); NHS allows twin co-bedding while small. Article states this and defers to midwife/doctor.
- Room-sharing duration: BIÖG says first year, HAS and AAP 6 months; both are stated as given.
- Emergency numbers: de 112 + Telefonseelsorge; fr 15/112 + 3114; pt SAMU 192 + CVV 188; tr 112; en 999/911.
