## Local vs global differences
- DE, FR, TR: no official ml-by-age table exists on the opened national pages. Numbers are AAP/CDC and labelled as US figures in body, lists and FAQ; local bodies are quoted only for what they say (signals, no pressure, manufacturer dosing).
- PT-BR: SBP table is the local reference; difference to AAP (4-5 feeds at 6 mo vs 2-3 after 6 mo) is stated. Ministério da Saúde does not recommend bottles; stated.
- TR: Bakanlık daily milk ranges apply to complementary-feeding age, not per feed; stated.
- CDC "need not finish the bottle" is a 6-23 month statement; scoped accordingly. For younger babies the support is CDC "take what they need ... stop when full", AAP "let them tell you", and DE/FR national pages.
