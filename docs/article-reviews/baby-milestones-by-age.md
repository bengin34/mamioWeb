## Local vs global differences
- fr: HAS corrected age until 2 y for <37 SA; CDC says >3 weeks early. Article states both.
- de/fr/pt-br/tr: local follow-up schedules (U1-U9, 20 examens, Caderneta, izlem) use their own age points and tools, not the CDC lists. Stated in text.
- pt-br, tr: no local milestone list was fetchable (SBP, Sağlık Bakanlığı protocol); article says local tool applies.

## Open questions for a clinician
- fr: confirm the current ameli month schedule for year 1 (two ameli pages disagree).
- pt-br: SBP reportedly corrects age to 2 y (3 y for extremely preterm per some literature); not opened, not stated in the article.
- tr: Ministry protocol (GİDR development tool) could not be opened; local section is generic.
