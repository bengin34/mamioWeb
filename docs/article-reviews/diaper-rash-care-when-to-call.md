## Local vs global differences
- Wipes: NHS and SBP allow fragrance-free/pH-balanced wipes; ameli.fr says drop wipes while irritated; Kinderärzte im Netz say avoid wipes. fr, de, pt-br follow local and say it differs.
- Barrier cream: NHS thin layer vs AAP/SBP thick layer on irritated skin. Stated in en, de, tr; pt-br uses SBP.
- Seek-care timing: ameli 3 days, AAP 2-3 days, Kinderärzte im Netz "early". Stated in fr.
- tr: practical detail from one provincial hospital page and a hospital NICU guide, no national page; red flags borrow NHS/AAP and are attributed.
