## Local vs global differences
- FR: ameli/HAS <=500 ppm and "trace" then pea at first molars vs rice-grain smear elsewhere. Stated. Both French pages are old-style (HAS 2010); a 2025 HAS update exists and was not opened.
- DE: 1000 ppm, rice grain to 24 mo (Gesund ins Leben) vs once-daily brushing (kindergesundheit-info) vs pediatrician tablets. Stated.
- BR: MS "less than a rice grain", 1000-1500 ppm; SBP rice grain, >=1100 ppm, pea from 2 y (US/UK pea from 3). Stated; SBP piece is from 2011.
- TR: two ministry pages disagree (no paste to 3 vs to 2; leblebi vs nohut) and both disagree with ADA/AAP/NHS (start at first tooth). Article reports all, decides nothing, tells parent to ask the dentist. Local pages are provincial dental centres; no national page found.
- "Spit, don't rinse" in de/fr/pt-br/tr rests on AAP/NHS only; articles say so openly. Nearest local statements (kindergesundheit-info: learn to spit ~4 y; ameli: teach to spit) added.
