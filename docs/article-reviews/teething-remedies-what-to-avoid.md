## Local vs global differences
- DE: kindergesundheit-info.de and BVKJ allow local-anaesthetic or pain-relieving gels; FDA/AAP advise against benzocaine/lidocaine. Article states both, advises asking about the active ingredient.
- DE: BVKJ calls fever and diarrhoea common and mostly harmless with teething; AAP/NHS do not link them. Article follows the cautious line and says so.
- FR: Santé.fr mentions soothing gels in pharmacies; ameli gives first tooth at 4 to 7 months.
- PT-BR: MS says sugar-free until 2 years; MS says fever/diarrhoea may or may not be teeth.
- TR: Tunceli lists cold foods, ice in a cloth and frozen bagel, and low fever/loose stools as teething signs; NHS/AAP are stricter on frozen items and on fever/diarrhoea. Article follows the stricter rule.
- AAP itself suggests a freezer-chilled washcloth; article advises the fridge and says so.
