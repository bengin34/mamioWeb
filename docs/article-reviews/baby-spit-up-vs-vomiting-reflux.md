## Local vs global differences
- Green vs yellow: NHS says green or yellow, AAP says green (bile); ameli.fr and SBP say bilious. Documented.
- Sleep incline: NHS and ameli.fr say flat, head not raised; SBP describes 30-40 degree elevation. Documented in pt-br; other locales cite NHS or ameli.fr only.
- Feed size: only AAP gives numbers; ameli.fr says smaller, more frequent meals; NHS silent. Documented.
- Fade age: NHS about 1 year, AAP about 7 months for many, ameli.fr before 1 year, SBP 90-95% by 1 year. Documented.

## Open questions for a clinician (if any)
