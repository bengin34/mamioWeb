## Local vs global differences
- de: LIFIB advice (no leg cycling before ~6 weeks) is British; German U3 is week 4-5 with hip ultrasound. Text says so, tells parents to ask at U3.
- fr: mpedia says belly massage relieves gas without evidence; stated as mpedia's claim, evidence called thin. Mpedia says start in first weeks; NHS source says 4-6 weeks; both shown.
- pt-br/tr: no national massage page with ages/oils/contraindications found (gov.br Shantala has no age/oil/contraindication text; Sağlık Bakanlığı pages found have no baby-massage guidance). Global guidance used and labelled. pt-br SBP fever page and tr TPD/TPK pages not openable/found.
- No local official source found for oils in de/fr/pt-br/tr beyond mpedia.

## Open questions for a clinician
- Mamio's own exercise guide offers massage "from one month" and bicycle legs "from birth" (src/seo-pages-new.js). Article now says (per LIFIB leaflet) no bicycling before ~6 weeks. Conflict; app copy or article needs a clinician decision. Article also does not state the in-app start ages.
- LIFIB is a multi-disciplinary regional group, NHS-hosted leaflet, not a national body; it is the only source for the ~6 week leg-cycling rule. Keep or drop?
- Tresillian (NSW Health-funded service) is the only source for stress/oils/essential-oil rules; Cochrane review is from 2013.
- Cambridge neonatal-unit leaflet (vaccination within 3 days) is for neonatal units; applied to home massage cautiously as "ask first".
- Section-level wording of "ask your health visitor" in pt-br/tr/de/fr uses local roles (pediatra, Hebamme/Kinderarztpraxis, sage-femme/médecin).

Verdict: publish after fixes (done). Do not skip the open question about Mamio app start ages vs the 6-week rule before promoting the article.
