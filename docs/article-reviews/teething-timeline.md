## Local vs global differences
- DE: BVKJ names fever above 39 C as a reason to see a doctor and calls diarrhoea a common teething companion; NHS/kindergesundheit-info treat 38 C fever and diarrhoea as not caused by teething. Article states both and follows the cautious line.
- FR: HAS/ameli recommend <=500 ppm under 3 and pea size after first molars; UK/US/DE use a rice-grain smear (DE at 1,000 ppm). Stated in text.
- FR: mpedia.fr mentions homeopathy; NHS and FDA do not recommend it. Stated in text.
- BR: MS pages give "less than a rice grain" and also 1,000-1,500 ppm; no first-visit age given, ADA timing quoted as ADA.
- TR: Tunceli ADSM suggests frozen items (frozen bagel, ice in cloth) and says loose stools can occur; NHS/FDA warn against frozen rings and NHS/AAP say no diarrhoea evidence. Article follows NHS/FDA for freezing and says so. No Turkish national brushing/fluoride guidance found; text says so.
- TR has only one local official source (a provincial ADSM page).
