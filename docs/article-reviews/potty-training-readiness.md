## Local vs global differences
- Timing: NHS 18 mo-2.5 y (no need to wait for signs); AAP signs 18-24 mo (lists signs); DE 2nd-3rd year (IQWiG: around 2nd birthday; BVKJ 2016: 2.5-3 y); FR 18-24 mo (mpedia) vs 2-3 y (ameli); BR 1.5 y (SBP) vs 2-3 y (SBP 2018); TR 18 mo stool / 24 mo bladder at earliest. Article states each as differing.
- Waiting for signs: NHS says do not wait; AAP/others describe signs. Stated as disagreement.
- When to delay: AAP and Fiocruz list events; NHS, kgi, ameli, mpedia do not. Article says so.
- Night: DE normal until 5; FR enuresis not before 5-6 / ameli after 6; stated as differing.
