## Local vs global differences
- Fridge: CDC 4 d, NHS 8 d (4C) / 3 d, DE 4 d, FR 48 h, BR 12 h, TR 4 d. Freezer: CDC 6-12 mo, NHS 6 mo, DE 6 (12) mo, FR 4 mo, BR 15 d, TR 6 mo (İslahiye 3 mo). Each language follows local pages and says others differ.
- Thawed milk: CDC 24 h, NHS straight away, DE 2 d, FR not stated, BR 12 h (SBP 24 h), TR 24 h.
- GiL itself notes "Muttermilch to go" leaflet gives slightly different, equally safe figures.
