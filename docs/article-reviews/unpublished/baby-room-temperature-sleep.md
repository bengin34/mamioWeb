## Local vs global differences
- Room temperature: UK 16-20, DE 18 (unverified here), FR 18-20, TR 20-22 (one hospital page; Behçet Uz says 21-22 for a feverish baby), BR no number. Each language states its local figure and says others differ.
- AAP says no water in first 6 months; ameli says offer water hourly to children. The FR text now flags this.

## Open questions for a clinician (if any)
- TR 20-22 C is a single hospital page and higher than UK/DE/FR; consider asking whether to present it as such.
- BR fever threshold (PE state page) and hydration advice (PR hospital, 2010) are state-level and not baby-specific.
- NHS "baby sleeping bags are safer than swaddling" appears on the page in a co-sleeping context; kept as the page words it.
