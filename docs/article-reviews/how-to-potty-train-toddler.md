## Local vs global differences
- Rewards: NHS allows stickers/charts; AAP says avoid treats. Article states the disagreement.
- Age: NHS 18 mo-2.5 y; AAP 2-3 y average start; Santé.fr 18-24 mo; ameli 2-3 y; SBP 1.5 y vs 2-3 y; Gevaş 18/24 mo earliest. Each language states its own sources' ranges.
- DE: kgi/BVKJ advise against starting early; article follows it.
- Emergency numbers: fr ameli "15 ou 112" (as on page). Other languages carry no emergency lists.
- BR and TR: no national step-by-step page found; article says so.

## Open questions for a clinician (if any)
- BR: no official red-flag list on opened SBP pages beyond the SBP/SBU thresholds (36/48 months).
- TR: only hospital/provincial Ministry pages; no national page.
- DE Probleme page concerns bedwetting (mainly 5+) and is applied to toddlers only for tone (no blame, praise).
- FR constipation red-flag lists come from pages written for constipation, not potty training; used only in the "when to consult" section.
