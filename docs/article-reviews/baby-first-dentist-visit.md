## Local vs global differences
- DE: Z1 6-9 months vs US "by first birthday". Article says DE follows local schedule; late first tooth: visit around first birthday.
- FR: ameli 6-12 months first visit; free M'T dents only from 3 to 24 years. Article states both and that the 6-12 month visit is not covered by M'T dents (cabinet conditions unspecified).
- UK: NHS pages give no first-visit age; article says so.
- BR: no official sign list; article says it follows NHS.
- TR: two ministry pages differ (first birthday or first tooth vs within 6 months of first tooth). Article presents both and suggests the earlier. No national ministry timetable found.
