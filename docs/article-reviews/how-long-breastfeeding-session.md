## Local vs global differences
- TR: local pages disagree among themselves (Siran: "not limited" and "at least 15 min"; Ozalp: until baby releases; 6-8 vs 5-6 urinations). Article says so. 15 min is not presented as a threshold.
- FR: SPF (10 min / 3/4-1 h) and Santé.fr (5 min / 30-45 min) differ; both shown, neither as a rule.
- DE: no minutes in German sources; NHS 5-40 min shown only as labelled England comparison.
- EN/NHS gives 5-40 min (one trust site); CDC/AAP/NHS national give none.

## Open questions for a clinician (if any)
- DE and PT-BR "when to ask" remain thin: the readable official local pages (kindergesundheit-info.de, Netzwerk Gesund ins Leben, gov.br, SBP) contain no red-flag list for feed length. Sections state this honestly and point to Hebamme/Kinderarzt/pediatra; no unsourced red flags were added. Verdict: publish after fixes (done). Caveat: TR and PT-BR rest on hospital pages / SBP only, and DE/PT-BR/TR red-flag coverage is thin by source limits; acceptable because nothing unsupported remains and sections tell readers to ask a professional.
