## Local vs global differences
- Drinks: NHS no cut-off; nidirect 1 h; kgi/gesund.bund.de 2 h; ameli/Santé.fr after 6 pm. Stated openly in every language.
- Night waking: NHS, kgi, ameli say avoid; gesund.bund.de lists brief waking as an option. Stated.
- Age: NHS no age; nidirect under 5 not a problem; DE/SBP/Santé.fr/Denizli 5+; ameli consult after 6.
- kgi says wetting depends only conditionally on drinking; article keeps drink advice as a trial and tells parents to ask the doctor.
- BR: no Ministério da Saúde page found; SBP used; article says so. TR: only provincial/hospital pages; article says so.
