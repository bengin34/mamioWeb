## Local vs global differences
- Start age: de 5th-7th month, fr 4-6 months (ameli), vs NHS ~6 months. Local follows local, stated in text.
- Meals/day: fr 4 meals at ~8 months (2 diversified); tr 2-3 (broşür, 6-8 sayfası) vs 2 (Çocuk Beslenmesi); en CDC 5-6 feeds incl. formula. No unified number invented.
- Water: CDC 4-8 oz/day vs AAP max 1 cup vs de "Wasser ab 3. Brei" without volume vs pt-br SBP 0,8 L total fluid. Stated side by side.
- Milk order: NHS milk after solids; AAP milk first; stated as a difference.
- Emergency numbers: none in this article (no red-flag list), none needed.
- Product claims: only the Product-facts style CTA (record meals, mark allergens, no account, local storage); one mention per language.

## Open questions for a clinician (if any)
- EN AAP "4 ounces at each daily meal" has no age on the page; kept with a hedge. Consider dropping it if a pediatrician finds it misleading for 6-month-olds.
- TR brochure lists fish and egg white as "not appropriate" at 6-8 months; article does not repeat it. Not an allergen-timing guide, left out on purpose.
