## Local vs global differences
- fr: ameli says exclusively breastfed babies can go 8-14 days; AAP says several days to a week; NHS asks for contact after 24-48 h in weeks 4 days-6 weeks. Article states the French figure as ameli's, adds the AAP <1 month caution.
- de: no fixed frequency rule (7/day to 1/week); article follows it.
- pt-br: SBP gives no day-count threshold; water <6 months is NHS only.
- tr: Turkish official pages give no frequency range or red-flag list for baby constipation; article says so and uses NHS/AAP.
- AAP symptom checker is Schmitt Pediatric Guidelines content hosted on HealthyChildren.org.
