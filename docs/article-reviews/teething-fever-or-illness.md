## Local vs global differences
- DE: fever threshold 38.5C (38C first weeks); fever >1 day up to ~2y, drinking refusal, no urine 12h, seizure -> Kinderarzt; 112 in doubt. Stricter than NHS for older babies. Stated in de body.
- FR: ameli urgent/same-day lists differ from NHS (40C, 2 days under 2y, 24h recurrence). Stated in fr body.
- BR: no Brazilian page with fever thresholds or red flags; ministry says fever/diarrhoea may or may not relate to teething; thresholds and red flags are NHS, stated in pt-br body.
- TR: ministry says <38C can accompany teething, diarrhoea link "very doubtful"; hospital page says any fever under 2y -> doctor without delay. Stated in tr body.
