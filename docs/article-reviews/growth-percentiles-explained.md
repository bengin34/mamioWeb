## Local vs global differences
- Birth weight regain: DE 7, at latest ~10 days; FR consult if not above birth weight at 10 days (mpedia); BR ~10th day (Caderneta); UK/US 2-3 weeks. Written as local guidance in each language, global figure kept alongside.
- Corrected age: AAP first 2 years; BR Caderneta to 2 years (WHO curve); TR MoH 2-3 years; DE DGSPJ paper for SPZ: whole childhood. Each language names its local source.
- Head circumference concern: RCPCH >99.6th centile only if rising after 6 months; BR Caderneta flags <-2 or >+2 z. Both stated in pt-br.
- Charts: DE/FR use national curves, BR/TR cite their ministry protocols, UK/US WHO-based.
- Weighing frequency (UK RCPCH) is stated as UK guidance only in en; de uses kindergesundheit-info.de, fr uses mpedia.fr.

## Open questions for a clinician
- Whether "a measurement outside the outer chart lines warrants a closer look" (en) should be tied to the CDC 2.3rd/97.7th flag for under-24-months or left generic; kept generic.
- Whether Mamio should state that percentiles use actual age in the WHO section as well as the preterm section; currently only the preterm section.
- RCPCH low-birthweight (under 32 weeks) chart claim is only visible on a chart PDF whose text was not fully opened; dropped from the article.
- The Brazilian source opened is the girls' Caderneta (8th ed., 2013); the boys' edition was not opened. The cited chart/z-score text is the same sex-neutral section.
- Turkey: the protocol does not state which exact curve set is printed in the child health record; the article says so and tells parents to ask.
